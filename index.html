I

Their story

Some things begin long before anyone calls them a beginning.

A lecture hall. Two sets of notes — one of them borrowed, often.

Then the rounds, the long shifts, and the longer nights.

II

The years between

Every journey home became the same journey.

The last bus. One umbrella. Neither of them in a hurry.

And always, without ever agreeing to it, the long way round.

The evenings

A coat on the handlebars. Two teas going cold.

The same stretch of sea, at the same hour, for years.

III

The question

One of them had been carrying it for a while.

Kept quiet for months. Asked once. Answered immediately.
